Making an Appointment

How to Book

By Phone

Call +27 33 395 4703 during business hours (08:00 - 16:00)

In Person

Visit the paediatric reception desk Monday to Friday

Referral

Get a referral from your doctor or clinic

What You Need

  • Child's Road to Health card
  • Parent/guardian ID
  • Medical aid card (if applicable)
  • Referral letter (for specialist clinics)
  • Previous medical records

Special Programs & Initiatives

👶

Well Baby Program

Comprehensive health monitoring for infants under 1 year, including growth tracking and developmental screening.

🎒

School Health Program

Annual health assessments for school-aged children, including vision, hearing, and immunization checks.

🍎

Nutrition Clinic

Monthly clinic for children with nutrition concerns, malnutrition, or feeding difficulties.

Important Information

Before Your Visit

  • • Arrive 15 minutes early for registration
  • • Bring all required documents
  • • Bring list of questions for the doctor
  • • Don't come if child has contagious illness

During Your Visit

  • • Follow clinic procedures and wait times
  • • Ask questions about your child's health
  • • Take notes on doctor's instructions
  • • Schedule follow-up appointments before leaving
