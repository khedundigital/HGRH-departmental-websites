Special Units

ICU / High Care

By special arrangement with nursing staff only. Limited visiting due to patient condition.

Neonatal Unit

Parents may visit anytime with staff permission. Limited to parents only.

Isolation Rooms

Restricted visiting due to infection control. Please check with nursing staff.

Important Visiting Rules

  • Maximum 2 visitors per patient at a time
  • No visitors under 12 years old
  • No visitors with colds or infectious diseases
  • Hand sanitization required on entry/exit
  • No food or drinks from outside
  • No flowers or plants in patient areas
  • Mobile phones on silent mode
  • Respect quiet hours (22:00 - 06:00)

Guide for Parents & Guardians

During Your Child's Stay

Stay Involved

Participate in your child's care routine and ask questions about treatment plans.

Keep Records

Maintain a diary of your child's progress and any questions for doctors.

Build Relationships

Get to know the nursing team caring for your child.

What You Can Do

Emotional Support

Provide comfort, reassurance, and normalcy for your child.

Advocate

Speak up if you have concerns about your child's care.

Prepare for Discharge

Learn about home care instructions and follow-up appointments.

Safety & Security

Infection Control

  • • Isolation protocols for infectious cases
  • • Staff trained in infection prevention

Security Measures
