Harry Gwala Regional Hospital

Harry Gwala Regional Hospital, formerly known as Edendale Hospital, is a prominent Regional/Teaching Hospital serving the Pietermaritzburg community and surrounding areas in KwaZulu-Natal.

As a teaching hospital affiliated with the Nelson R. Mandela School of Medicine, we are committed to excellence in patient care, medical education, and research.

Teaching & Training

As a satellite campus of the Nelson R. Mandela School of Medicine, we are proud to contribute to the education and training of the next generation of healthcare professionals. Our department serves as a training ground for medical students, interns, and resident doctors.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, compassionate, and high-quality healthcare services to all children in our community, regardless of their background or circumstances. We strive to create a healing environment that promotes the physical, emotional, and social well-being of our young patients.

Our Vision

To be the leading paediatric care center in the region, recognized for our clinical excellence, innovative approaches to child healthcare, and our commitment to medical education and research.
