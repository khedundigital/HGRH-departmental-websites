🚗 By Car

Located on Selby Msimang Road in Edendale. Free parking available on-site. Follow signs to the main entrance and then to the Paediatrics Department.

🚌 Public Transport

Several local bus routes stop near the hospital. Ask for the Harry Gwala Hospital stop on Selby Msimang Road.

🚑 Ambulance Entrance

Emergency vehicles should use the dedicated ambulance entrance for immediate access to the emergency department.
