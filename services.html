Emergency Paediatrics

24/7 Emergency Care

Our emergency paediatric service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to handle urgent medical situations. We provide rapid assessment and treatment for acute childhood illnesses and injuries.

When to Visit Emergency:

  • • Difficulty breathing or severe asthma attack
  • • High fever with rash or seizures
  • • Major injuries or accidents
  • • Severe dehydration or vomiting
  • • Any life-threatening condition

🚨 EMERGENCY: Call +27 33 395 4703 for immediate assistance
