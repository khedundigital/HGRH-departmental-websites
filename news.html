Updated Visiting Hours for Paediatric Ward Effective Immediately

November 28, 2024 • Hospital Administration

Due to increased patient load and infection control measures, Harry Gwala Regional Hospital has revised the visiting hours for the paediatric ward. The new schedule aims to balance patient rest with family access while ensuring safety for all.

New Visiting Hours:

  • Monday - Friday: 15:00 - 16:00 and 19:00 - 20:00
  • Weekends & Public Holidays: 15:00 - 16:00
  • ICU/High Care: By special arrangement only
