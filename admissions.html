Paediatric Admissions

Everything you need to know about admitting your child to our care

🚨 For Medical Emergencies: Proceed directly to our Emergency Department or Call +27 33 395 4703

Admission Process

1

Referral

Get a referral from your doctor or come through emergency

2

Assessment

Medical evaluation to determine need for admission

3

Registration

Complete admission forms and provide documentation

4

Admission

Child is admitted to the appropriate ward for care

When Admission is Required

Severe Illness

High fever, dehydration, severe respiratory distress, or other acute conditions requiring 24/7 monitoring

Surgical Procedures

Children requiring surgery or post-operative care

IV Treatment

Conditions requiring intravenous medications or fluids

Observation

Children requiring close medical observation and monitoring

Chronic Conditions

Acute exacerbations of chronic illnesses like asthma or diabetes

Referral Guidelines

For Healthcare Professionals

When referring patients to our paediatric department:

Emergency Referrals

Call +27 33 395 4703 to speak with the paediatrician on duty

Elective Admissions

Fax referral to: [Fax Number] with patient details and clinical summary

Required Information

  • • Patient demographic details
  • • Clinical history and examination findings
  • • Investigations results
  • • Reason for referral
  • • Urgency level

Transfer Arrangements

Discuss with receiving doctor before transfer. Ensure patient stability.

Required Documents

Essential Documents

  • Child's birth certificate or ID document
  • Parent/guardian ID document
  • Referral letter from referring doctor
  • Medical aid card (if applicable)
  • Previous medical records

Additional Information

  • List of current medications
  • Known allergies
  • Immunization record (Road to Health card)
  • Contact details of emergency person
  • Proof of income (for indigent patients)

Important Guidelines for Parents

During Admission

  • Stay with your child as much as possible
  • Participate in your child's care plan
  • Ask questions about treatment and medications
  • Inform staff of any changes in your child's condition

Visiting Rules

  • Follow designated visiting hours strictly
  • Limited visitors (2 per patient)
  • No visitors with colds or infectious diseases
  • Children under 12 may need special permission
