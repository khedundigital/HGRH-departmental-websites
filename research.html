Our Research Mission

As a teaching hospital affiliated with the Nelson R. Mandela School of Medicine, we are committed to advancing paediatric healthcare through research and quality improvement initiatives. Our focus areas include:

  • Clinical research to improve treatment outcomes
  • Quality improvement projects to enhance patient care
  • Audits to monitor and improve clinical standards
  • Training future healthcare researchers

Quality Improvement Initiatives

Reducing Hospital Readmissions

Implementing discharge planning and follow-up protocols to reduce 30-day readmission rates for asthma and gastroenteritis.

Target: 25% reduction in readmissions | Timeline: 12 months | Status: Implementation Phase

Emergency Department Triage

Optimizing triage processes to reduce waiting times and improve patient flow in the paediatric emergency department.

Target: 30% reduction in wait times | Timeline: 6 months | Status: Data Collection

Medication Safety

Enhancing medication safety through electronic prescribing and barcode medication administration.

Target: Zero medication errors | Timeline: 18 months | Status: Planning Phase

Patient Experience

Improving patient and family satisfaction through enhanced communication and family-centered care initiatives.

Target: 90% satisfaction rate | Timeline: Ongoing | Status: Implementation Phase

"Impact of Rapid HIV Testing on Time to Initiation of Antiretroviral Therapy in Infants"

Authors: Brown L, Davis M, Miller S, et al. | Journal: Paediatrics and International Child Health | Year: 2024

Prospective study demonstrating 40% reduction in time to ART initiation following implementation of point-of-care HIV testing in the paediatric ward.

Research Ethics & Governance

Our Commitment

All research conducted at Harry Gwala Regional Hospital adheres to strict ethical standards and regulatory requirements. We are committed to:

  • Protecting participant rights and welfare
  • Ensuring informed consent processes
  • Maintaining confidentiality and data protection
  • Following Good Clinical Practice guidelines

Ethics Committee

Our research is reviewed and approved by:

KwaZulu-Natal Provincial Health Research Ethics Committee

All protocols undergo rigorous review before implementation. The committee ensures compliance with national and international research ethics standards.

Institutional Review Board

Harry Gwala Regional Hospital maintains an active IRB that oversees all research activities within the institution.
