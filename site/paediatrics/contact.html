Which part of the hospital?

  1. Use the published address to locate the hospital.
  2. Before travelling, call the main hospital telephone and ask which Paediatrics clinic, admissions point or ward applies to your visit.
  3. On arrival, follow your appointment or referral instructions and ask hospital staff if the destination is unclear.

The previous website named a “Main Building, Level 2 – Paediatrics Wing” and a paediatric reception desk. Those exact internal locations have not been confirmed and are not directions for patients.

General hospital visiting times

The official hospital page lists Monday to Friday: 13:00–14:00 and 17:00–18:00; weekends and public holidays: 13:00–15:00.

Confirm Paediatrics ward rules first. The previous prototype listed different times; neither page establishes a current paediatric ward policy.

Urgent concerns

This prototype cannot triage a child. Seek local emergency care if urgent help is needed. Do not use the hospital switchboard or this site as an emergency response service.
