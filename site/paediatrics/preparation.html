Paediatrics · patient journey

Prepare for a visit

A printable set of questions to help you confirm arrangements. This is not a hospital-issued checklist or clinical instruction.

Use with your healthcare team

Questions to check before you leave

  • Where should we go, and at what time?
  • Is this visit booked, referred or a walk-in? What proof is needed?
  • Which identification, appointment information or existing records should we bring?
  • Should we bring a list of the child's current medicines or other information?
  • Is there anything specific we should do before this visit?

These prompts are generic. The answers must come from the responsible healthcare team or official hospital channel.

Keep your own details private

Do not enter names, medical details or contact information on this demonstration website. It has no booking, contact or feedback form.

Print this page

Use your browser's Print command to take these questions with you. The print layout keeps the demonstration warning visible.
