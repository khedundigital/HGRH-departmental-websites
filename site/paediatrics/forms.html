Printable forms & help

Get ready to ask for support.

Find official South African grant information, printable SASSA supporting PDFs and a simple preparation checklist. This site cannot assess eligibility or submit an application.

Application form availability

Ask SASSA for the actual application form.

South African Government says the Care Dependency Grant and Disability Grant application forms are not available online; they are obtained at a SASSA office and completed with a SASSA officer. The PDFs below are supporting forms, not the application itself.
