Helpful reading to keep.

Download original WHO PDFs for family and caregiver learning, or open the publisher’s page to check the latest edition. Ask your own healthcare team which material is right for your child.

About these resources: they are published by WHO, not by this prototype. The guides below have different audiences; their inclusion is not a hospital recommendation or individual clinical advice. WHO has not endorsed this site.
