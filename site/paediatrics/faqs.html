Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what this demonstration can and cannot confirm.

Access and preparation

How do I arrange a Paediatrics visit?

This prototype cannot arrange visits. The appropriate route, referral requirement and contact details have not been verified. Follow instructions from your healthcare team and check the official KZN Health hospital page for current information.

What should I bring?

Requirements may vary. Ask the hospital or your healthcare team which documents, appointment information and existing records apply to your visit. The preparation prompts can help you ask.

Where do I go when I arrive?

No ward, clinic or admissions desk location has been approved for this site. Confirm the correct location before travelling or ask official hospital staff on arrival.

What are the clinic or visiting hours?

Current departmental hours have not been verified for this prototype. Check the official hospital channel for the latest arrangements.

Safety and website status

Can I book an appointment or send a question here?

No. This demonstration does not accept personal information, send messages or make bookings.

Is this an official hospital website?

No. It is an independent design prototype and has not been approved as a hospital information service.

Is an isiZulu version available?

A draft isiZulu demonstration is available through the language switch on every page. It is AI-assisted and awaits qualified translation and departmental clinical review. It must not be treated as an approved patient resource.

Can I join the premature baby mum support group?

No. It is a programme proposal for discussion, not a running group or enrolment service.

Can I download a disability or care grant application here?

Official SASSA supporting PDFs and a printable checklist are on the forms page. The actual grant application form must be obtained at a SASSA office, according to South African Government guidance.

What if I need urgent help?

This site cannot assess a child or provide emergency triage. Seek local emergency care immediately when you believe urgent help is needed.
