Admissions & access

Understand the proposed admission journey.

The earlier website mapped a simple sequence from referral to admission. It is a useful demonstration of how the process could be explained, but the department has not confirmed current instructions.

Historical prototype sequence

Four steps shown in the earlier design

  1. ReferralOriginal example: a doctor’s referral or arrival through emergency care.
  2. AssessmentOriginal example: a hospital team evaluates whether admission is needed.
  3. RegistrationOriginal example: paperwork and documents are checked.
  4. AdmissionOriginal example: the child is directed to an appropriate ward.

These are historical design examples, not verified instructions or admission criteria.

Documents the old design suggested asking about

Identity and appointment

  • Child’s birth certificate or ID
  • Parent or guardian ID
  • Referral or appointment letter, if applicable

Health records

  • Road to Health card
  • Previous medical records
  • Medicine and allergy information

Ask staff which items actually apply to your visit; this is not a mandatory hospital list.

Times from the earlier prototype

Unverified: the older page described planned admissions Monday–Friday, 08:00–16:00, and emergency admissions through the Emergency Department at any time. This demonstration does not confirm those hours or an emergency contact line. For urgent help, seek local emergency care.

Before travelling, use instructions given to you by your healthcare team. For the current hospital contact and address, see Contact & directions.
