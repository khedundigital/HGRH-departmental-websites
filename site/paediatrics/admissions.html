Admissions and access

A demonstration of how a caregiver could understand the next step, with operational details clearly marked for hospital confirmation.

Before travelling, confirm the pathway

The hospital has not verified the admission or referral route for this page. If you have a current appointment, referral or instruction, follow what your healthcare team gave you and confirm any uncertainty through official hospital channels.

Hospital confirmation required: admission criteria, referral route, documents needed, opening times, ward or desk location and current contact details.

A proposed caregiver journey

  1. Identify why you are visiting. Is it a planned visit, a follow-up, or an immediate concern? The relevant hospital team must provide the correct route.
  2. Check the latest instructions. Use your existing appointment or referral information and the hospital's official contact channel.
  3. Ask what to bring. Confirm any documents, medicines or records with staff before travelling.
  4. Know who to ask on arrival. The correct desk or location must be supplied and verified by the hospital.

If your child needs urgent help

This site cannot assess urgency or provide emergency triage. Seek local emergency care immediately when you believe your child needs urgent help. Do not wait for a reply from this prototype.
