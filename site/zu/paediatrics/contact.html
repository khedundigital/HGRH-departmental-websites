Ikheli nokuxhumana

Ikheli nezinombolo ezingezansi zishicilelwe yi-KZN Health zesibhedlela sonke. Indawo ethile yomnyango wezingane isadinga ukuqinisekiswa.

Ikheli elisemthethweni

Harry Gwala Regional Hospital

Lot 89, Selby Msimang Road
Plessislaer, Pietermaritzburg
KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

Izinombolo zesibhedlela sonke

Shayela ukuze uqinisekise

Inombolo enkulu: +27 (0) 33 395 4911

Isikhulu sobudlelwano nomphakathi: 033 395 4020

Lezi azibhalwanga njengezinombolo zokubhuka noma zezimo eziphuthumayo zomnyango wezingane.

Ingabe umnyango wezingane ukuphi?

Iwebhusayithi endala yayithi “Main Building, Level 2 – Paediatrics Wing”. Le ndawo ayikaqinisekiswa, ngakho ayisona isiqondiso sokuhamba. Shayela inombolo enkulu ubuze indawo efanele isikhathi sakho.

Ikhasi elisemthethweni linikeza izikhathi zokuvakasha zesibhedlela sonke. Qinisekisa imithetho yewadi lezingane ngaphambi kokuvakasha.

Lena iwebhusayithi yesibonelo ezimele. Ayikagunyazwa yisibhedlela. Ayigcini ulwazi lweziguli futhi ayibhukhi noma ibhalise abantu.
