Isibonelo sokunakekelwa kwezingane

Izibonelo zemitholampilo

Ithebula elingezansi livela kuwebhusayithi endala. Akusilo uhlelo lwamanje oluqinisekisiwe. Ungalisebenzisi ukuhlela ukuvakasha.

Umthombo osemthethweni

I-KZN Health ibala ukunakekelwa kwezingane njengenye yezinsiza zesibhedlela. Ayinikezi lolu hlelo lwemitholampilo.
