Lungiselela ukuvakasha

Leli khasi linikeza imibuzo ongayibuza, hhayi uhlu lwemithetho yesibhedlela.

Ngaphambi kokuhamba

  1. Qinisekisa indawo, usuku nesikhathi usebenzisa imiyalelo yakho yokuqokwa noma yabadokotela.
  2. Buza abasebenzi ukuthi yimiphi imibhalo namarekhodi okumele uwaphathe.
  3. Bhala imibuzo yakho ukuze uyibuze ngesikhathi sokuvakasha.
  4. Buza ukuthi ubani okufanele umbone uma ufika.
