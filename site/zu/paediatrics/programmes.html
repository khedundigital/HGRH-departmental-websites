Akukho mininingwane eqoqwayo

Buza abasebenzi besibhedlela

Uma ufuna ukwazi ukuthi yiluphi usizo lwabazali olukhona manje, buza esibhedlela. Ungathumeli ulwazi lomndeni kule webhusayithi.

Mayelana nalesi sibonelo

Lena iwebhusayithi yesibonelo ezimele. Ayikagunyazwa yisibhedlela. Ayigcini ulwazi lweziguli futhi ayibhukhi noma ibhalise abantu.
