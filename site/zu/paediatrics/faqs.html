Isibonelo sokunakekelwa kwezingane

Imibuzo evamile

Izimpendulo zikhombisa isimo salesi sibonelo. Qinisekisa imininingwane yamanje nesibhedlela.

Ngiyaphi uma ngifika?

Ikheli lesibhedlela liku ikhasi lokuxhumana. Indawo ethile yomnyango wezingane ayikaqinisekiswa; buza abasebenzi noma sebenzisa imiyalelo yesikhathi sakho.

Ingabe uhlelo lwemitholampilo lusekhona?

Ithebula lemitholampilo livela kuwebhusayithi endala futhi alimakiwe njengohlelo lwamanje. Qinisekisa nosibhedlela.

Ngingabhalisela iqembu lokweseka omama bezinsana ezizalwe ngaphambi kwesikhathi?

Cha. Lelo qembu lisewumqondo, alikavulwa futhi ayikho indlela yokubhalisa kule webhusayithi.

Ngingalitholaphi ifomu lesibonelelo?

Ikhasi lamafomu nosizo lixhumanisa amafomu asekelayo e-SASSA. Ifomu lesicelo uqobo litholakala ehhovisi le-SASSA ngokusho kukahulumeni.

Ingabe lolu hlelo lusemthethweni?

Cha. Lena iwebhusayithi yesibonelo engakagunyazwa yisibhedlela.

Udinga ulwazi lwamanje?

Bheka ikhasi elisemthethweni lesibhedlela noma ubuze abasebenzi.

Mayelana nalesi sibonelo

Lena iwebhusayithi yesibonelo ezimele. Ayikagunyazwa yisibhedlela. Ayigcini ulwazi lweziguli futhi ayibhukhi noma ibhalise abantu.
