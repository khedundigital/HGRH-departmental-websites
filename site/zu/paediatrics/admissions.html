Isibonelo sokunakekelwa kwezingane

Ukwamukelwa esibhedlela

Iwebhusayithi endala yayibonisa izinyathelo ezine. Lezi yizibonelo zangaphambilini, hhayi imiyalelo eqinisekisiwe.

Izinyathelo ezazikulesi sibonelo

  1. UkudluliselwaIsibonelo sangaphambilini: incwadi kadokotela noma ukufika ngosizo oluphuthumayo.
  2. UkuhlolwaIsibonelo: abasebenzi bahlola isidingo sokungeniswa.
  3. UkubhaliswaIsibonelo: kuhlolwa imibhalo edingekayo.
  4. UkungeniswaIsibonelo: ingane iqondiswa ewadini elifanele.

Imibhalo eyayiphakanyisiwe

Isitifiketi sokuzalwa noma i-ID yengane; i-ID yomzali noma umnakekeli; incwadi yokudluliselwa uma ikhona; i-Road to Health card; amarekhodi adlule. Buza abasebenzi ukuthi yini edingekayo kuwe.

Izikhathi ezazikulesi sibonelo

Akukaqinisekiswa: ikhasi elidala lalithi ukungeniswa okuhleliwe ngoMsombuluko kuya kuLwesihlanu, 08:00–16:00; usizo oluphuthumayo nge-Emergency Department. Leli khasi aliqinisekisi lezi zikhathi.

Uma udinga usizo oluphuthumayo, funa usizo oluphuthumayo endaweni yakini. Bheka ikhasi lokuxhumana ngenombolo enkulu yesibhedlela.

Isimo solwazi

Lezi zibonelo zivela kuwebhusayithi endala. Isibhedlela asikaqinisekisi indlela yamanje yokwamukelwa.

Mayelana nalesi sibonelo

Lena iwebhusayithi yesibonelo ezimele. Ayikagunyazwa yisibhedlela. Ayigcini ulwazi lweziguli futhi ayibhukhi noma ibhalise abantu.
