Referral Guidelines

When to Refer Urgently

Red Flag Symptoms

  • • Respiratory distress or cyanosis
  • • Altered level of consciousness
  • • Severe dehydration or shock
  • • Persistent fever >39°C for >3 days
  • • Seizures (first or prolonged)
  • • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • • Signs of meningitis

Emergency Contact: Call +27 33 395 4703 and ask for the paediatrician on duty

Routine Referral Criteria

Growth Concerns

  • • Weight loss or failure to thrive
  • • Crossing percentiles on growth chart

Developmental Delay

  • • Missed developmental milestones
  • • Loss of previously acquired skills

Chronic Conditions

  • • Poorly controlled asthma/diabetes
  • • Recurrent hospitalizations
