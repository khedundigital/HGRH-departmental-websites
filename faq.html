Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our paediatric services

Our visiting hours are:

  • Weekdays: 15:00 - 16:00 and 19:00 - 20:00
  • Weekends & Public Holidays: 15:00 - 16:00
  • ICU/High Care: As arranged with the nursing staff

Please note that visiting hours may be restricted during disease outbreaks or infection control periods.

Please bring the following items:

Essential Documents:

  • • Child's birth certificate or ID
  • • Parent/guardian ID
  • • Medical aid card (if applicable)
  • • Referral letter

For Your Child:

  • • Comfortable clothing
  • • Toiletries (toothbrush, etc.)
  • • Favorite blanket or toy
  • • Current medication list

Seek immediate emergency care for:

  • • Difficulty breathing or severe asthma attacks
  • • High fever with rash or seizures/convulsions
  • • Major injuries, burns, or accidents
  • • Severe dehydration (no tears, dry mouth, few wet diapers)
  • • Loss of consciousness or confusion
  • • Any life-threatening condition

Emergency Contact: +27 33 395 4703 (Available 24/7)

Medical Aid Patients:

We accept most major medical aid schemes. Please bring your medical aid card and pre-authorization when available. Some services may require pre-approval from your scheme.

Cash/Private Patients:

Yes, we welcome cash patients. Payment is required at the time of service. We offer competitive rates and payment plans can be arranged for larger amounts.

Government Patients:

As a public hospital, we provide services to all patients. Services are charged according to the national health tariff scale based on income level.

Booking Methods:

  • • Phone: Call +27 33 395 4703 during business hours
  • • In Person: Visit the paediatric reception
  • • Referral: Your doctor can refer you directly

What to Bring:

  • • Referral letter (if you have one)
  • • ID document
  • • Medical aid card
  • • Previous medical records

Meals are provided for all hospitalized children:

  • • Specialized paediatric menus designed by dietitians
  • • Accommodations for dietary restrictions and allergies
  • • Meal times: Breakfast (7:30), Lunch (12:30), Supper (17:30)
  • • Snacks available between meals
  • • Breastfeeding support available for mothers

You may bring preferred snacks or meals for your child, but please check with nursing staff first, especially if your child has special dietary needs.

  • • All current medications in original packaging
  • • A complete list of medications and dosages
  • • Any chronic medication your child takes regularly
  • • Allergy medications (inhalers, epipens, etc.)

Important: Do not give your child any medication while in hospital without consulting the treating doctor. The hospital pharmacy will provide necessary medications during the stay.

Before discharge, you will receive:

Documentation:

  • • Discharge summary
  • • Prescription for medications
  • • Follow-up appointment details
  • • Care instructions

Medication:

  • • 2-3 days supply of essential meds
  • • Instructions for home care
  • • Side effects to watch for
  • • When to seek further care

Discharge usually happens by 10:00 AM. Payment must be settled before leaving. The nurse will explain everything and answer any questions.
