# Content and release approval

This public GitHub Pages output is an **unofficial demonstration**. Before any hospital-endorsed release, the owner must secure written authority from hospital management/communications and named clinical and operational reviewers. Record each item in `content-register.csv` with source, reviewer, review date, approval status, owner and next review date. Keep approval evidence in a controlled non-public location; this public repository holds references, not personal or patient records. Verify phone numbers, addresses, admission/referral pathways, hours, service scope and clinical language directly with the responsible departments. A qualified isiZulu translator and a clinical reviewer must approve every translated patient-facing item. Recheck information on a defined schedule and after service changes. Do not remove the demonstration warning until authorised governance explicitly approves a replacement status and domain.
