# Design findings applied

GCIS places important tasks high on the page, recommends responsive CSS, restrained colour, clear hierarchy and meaningful graphics ([design and layout](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/design)). Its [information architecture](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/information-architecture) and [navigation guidance](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/navigation) favour citizen tasks, plain labels, shallow pathways, breadcrumbs, descriptive links and visible keyboard focus. The [common look and feel guidance](https://www.gcis.gov.za/guidelines/website/common-look-feel) calls for consistency, mobile adaptation and at least 4.5:1 normal-text contrast. Because this is an unofficial prototype, official emblems, flag banners and hospital branding are intentionally omitted.

The [KZN Health hospital page](https://www.kznhealth.gov.za/provincial-hospitals/harry-gwala-hospital/) and [services list](https://www.kznhealth.gov.za/provincial-hospitals/harry-gwala-hospital/harry-gwala-services/) establish a credible official destination and the broad existence of a paediatric service. They do not validate the prototype's admissions process, clinic details or emergency number. The page itself timed out in the research browser; the official-domain indexed result was consulted. The [NHS design system](https://service-manual.nhs.uk/design-system) and [GOV.UK design system](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/) inform card, callout, typography and disclosure patterns only. No third-party visual identity or image is copied.
