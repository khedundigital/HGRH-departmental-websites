# Paediatrics content verification queue

The public pages use only the broad paediatric service category from the official KZN Health services list and generic navigation/visit prompts. Before a hospital-authorised version, named reviewers must confirm the full service scope, admission and referral route, clinic and visiting arrangements, locations, contacts, emergency wording, document requirements and FAQ answers. Clinical and operational review evidence is not held in this public repository. See `docs/governance/content-register.csv` and `approval-workflow.md`.
